Our Mission

At Prodizy Tech, our mission is to make professional-grade digital solutions accessible to businesses of all sizes — from local startups in Kathmandu to growing companies across South Asia. We build websites, apps, and marketing strategies that create real, measurable impact for our clients.

We envision a Nepal where local businesses compete on a global digital stage. Our goal is to be the go-to technology partner for companies that want modern, well-built digital products — without the steep costs of international agencies or the unreliability of freelance work.

Our Story

Prodizy Tech was founded in 2023 by a group of friends who shared a frustration: too many Nepali businesses had outdated websites, non-existent social media, and no digital strategy — while simultaneously missing out on real revenue. We set out to change that.

What started as a small team taking on freelance web projects in Kathmandu has grown into a focused agency with a full roster of developers, designers, and digital marketers. We've worked with trekking companies, restaurants, handicraft exporters, educational institutions, and tech startups — each with a unique set of goals and constraints.

Today, Prodizy Tech operates as a lean, hands-on team that genuinely cares about outcomes. We don't overpromise. We build things that work, explain what we're doing in plain language, and stick around after launch to make sure everything runs smoothly.
